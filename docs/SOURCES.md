# RentRight – Legal Sources

This document records the official legal and government sources used by RentRight to collect tenancy-related information for Tamil Nadu.

## 1. Source Register

| ID | Source | Format | Topic / Information | Terms of Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-01 | Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act, 2017 | PDF / Legislation | Rights and responsibilities of landlords and tenants, rent, security deposit, tenancy agreements and eviction | Public law. Cite the relevant section number. |
| SRC-02 | Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Rules, 2019 | PDF / Government Rules | Registration procedures, prescribed forms and tenancy regulations | Public government document. Cite the relevant rule number. |
| SRC-03 | Model Tenancy Act, 2021 | PDF / Model Legislation | Model framework for tenancy agreements, rent, security deposits and dispute resolution | Reference material only. Not automatically applicable as Tamil Nadu law. |
| SRC-04 | Tamil Nadu Tenancy Portal | Web Page | Tenancy registration, Rent Authority information, FAQs and government guidance | Public government guidance. Verify against applicable legislation. |
| SRC-05 | India Code | Web Page / PDF | Central and state legislation, Acts and amendments | Public legislative resource. Cite the Act and section number. |
| SRC-06 | TNREGINET | Web Portal | Property registration, stamp duty and registration fee information | Government portal. Verify applicable rates and provisions before use. |

## 3. Data Structure

RentRight will organize extracted legal information using the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| id | Unique identifier for each legal record |
| section | Relevant section or rule number |
| topic | Subject of the legal provision |
| obligation_of | Person responsible for the obligation (landlord, tenant or both) |
| limit | Legal limit, duration or prescribed amount |
| penalty | Penalty or consequence specified by the provision |

## 4. Source Verification Guidelines

- Prioritize applicable Tamil Nadu legislation when providing legal information about Tamil Nadu tenancies.
- Use the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 as a reference framework and not as directly applicable Tamil Nadu law.
- Verify amendments and updated legal provisions before extracting information.
- Every legal obligation must reference its relevant section or rule number.
- Do not assume penalties, limits or obligations that are not explicitly mentioned in the source.
- Verify stamp duty and registration charges using the applicable government provisions.
- Record the document format (PDF, webpage, table or scanned document) for every source.

## 5. Purpose

These sources will form the legal reference base for RentRight. The information extracted from them will help identify landlord and tenant obligations, financial limits, registration requirements and applicable penalties.
